# Orbital Support Cannon

**In development**

You've emailed a company for help and the reply answered a different question. You've watched your ticket get closed while the problem stayed open. That's support measured by how fast tickets close.

Mapleset LLC is building Orbital Support Cannon to do it the other way around. It's a hosted, email-first support service for teams, judged by the problems it solves and the trust it builds.

## What we're planning

- **One shared inbox:** your team's customer email in one place.
- **Clear ownership:** assign each conversation, so everyone knows who's answering.
- **Team notes:** talk a conversation over beside it, without replying to the customer.
- **Live updates:** new messages and replies show up as they happen.

## Why it exists

Support is usually treated as a cost to cut. Judge it by ticket counts and response times, and people learn to close tickets instead of solving problems. Every support conversation either builds trust or spends it. Trust is an asset.

We want a tool where solving the problem is the easy path.
