# About Mapleset LLC

You've probably watched software you rely on get worse. The app that worked fine starts nagging you to upgrade. A feature you used moves to a pricier plan. Your data turns into someone else's business model. There's a word for it: enshittification.

Mapleset LLC is an independent software company, and we want to make software that doesn't go that way.

- **We build what we need.** Each product starts with a problem we had. We work on Mac, Windows and Linux computers, so we're building Deskwalker. We wanted line art from our 3D scenes, so we're building Senga Studio.
- **We use it ourselves.** Our tools are for our own work too, so their problems are our problems.
- **We want it to stay good.** Software gets worse when the company behind it needs more from its users than it gives back. We want a business where doing right by customers is also what keeps it, and the people who work here, going for the long run. We're still working out the details, so we'll only promise what we know we can keep.

Our projects, all in development:

- [Deskwalker](/deskwalker): one keyboard, mouse and clipboard for your Mac, Windows and Linux computers.
- [Senga Studio](/senga-studio): draws line art from 3D scenes.
- [Orbital Support Cannon](/orbital-support-cannon): email support for teams who see support as a competitive advantage, not a cost center.
