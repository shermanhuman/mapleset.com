# Deskwalker

**In development for macOS, Windows and Linux**

Two computers on one desk usually means two keyboards, two mice and a clipboard that stops at the screen edge. Mapleset LLC is building Deskwalker for desks like that. You'll move the pointer past the edge of one screen and keep working on the next machine, with the same keyboard and mouse. Copy on the Mac, paste on the Linux box. We started it because we work across Mac, Windows and Linux machines every day.

## What we're planning

- **Screen layout:** an editor for arranging your screens to match your desk.
- **Shared clipboard:** copy text or images on one computer and paste them on another.
- **Steady input:** keyboard and mouse on their own channel, meant to keep a large copy from holding up your typing.
- **Key mappings:** settings for each computer, so Ctrl and Cmd land where you expect.
- **Private connections:** computers will pair with each other, and we plan to encrypt the traffic between them.
