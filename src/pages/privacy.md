# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

This policy covers visits to the Mapleset LLC website and email correspondence with us. We don't sell personal information.

## This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are self-hosted, and the site makes no third-party requests.

We don't collect personal information through the site. Our host, GitHub Pages, may log technical information, including IP addresses, for security and operations. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Our products

Deskwalker, Senga Studio and Orbital Support Cannon are in development. Before each is released, we'll add a section explaining what information it uses, where it goes and how to remove it.

## Email

We use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of our correspondence.

When this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
